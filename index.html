Who am I ?

Hi, I'm SowaBerry Aka Sowal. I'm an aspiring artist with a passion for exploration, military themes, and "Gijinka" (humanization), though lately, I find myself leaning more toward Kemonomimi designs. I constantly strive to explore new possibilities in visual storytelling by bringing characters with unique identities to reality. I am dedicated to continuously learning and honing my skills whether it's exploring 3D modeling, collaborating on various projects, or exchanging knowledge and experiences with others. From what I've learned, art is far more than just creating a final piece; it is a tool for discovering new things and forging meaningful connections between ourselves, our work, and the people around us
